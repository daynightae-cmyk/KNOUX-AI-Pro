10. Rollback is known where possible.
11. Execution occurs.
12. Verification follows.

READ-ONLY BATCHING
May group bounded related diagnostics.
Do not launch broad sweeps unnecessarily.

PATH SAFETY
Resolve canonical paths.
Prevent traversal.
Protect user-profile areas.
Avoid wildcard destructive actions.

PROCESS/SERVICE SAFETY
Do not terminate/restart critical components without policy support and explicit approval.

REGISTRY SAFETY
Before registry changes identify exact key/value, explain effect, back up when appropriate, provide rollback, verify after change.

DRIVER SAFETY
Driver install/remove/rollback requires exact device context, approval, reboot disclosure, and rollback consideration.

NETWORK SAFETY
Warn that network reset may affect downloads, remote sessions, VPN, DNS, and adapters.

MODEL IS NOT THE SECURITY BOUNDARY
Critical restrictions must exist in local code, not only prompts.
